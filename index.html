🚇 Tube Router

Privacy Policy

✓ No personal data collected

Overview

Tube Router is a London Underground journey planner built for simplicity and privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. No account is required to use the app.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No name, email address, or contact details
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No cookies or cross-app tracking

TfL API

Journey search queries are sent directly to the Transport for London Open Data API to retrieve route and line status information. These requests are made directly from your device to TfL's servers. Tube Router does not log, intercept, or store these queries. Please refer to TfL's Privacy Policy for information on how TfL handles API requests.

In-App Purchases

Tube Router offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock Pro features. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple via the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Purchase records are managed by your Apple ID.

Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your favourite routes (if Pro is unlocked)
  • Recent journey searches
  • Your Pro purchase status

This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Children's Privacy

Tube Router does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
